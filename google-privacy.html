Deenox Plancia – Privacy Policy (Google data)

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Deenox Plancia is the desktop part of Deenox, a personal assistant made and used by Diego Fanni, known as Doctor Dee (Italy). This policy explains what Google data Deenox Plancia uses when it connects to Gmail and Google Calendar.

Who uses Deenox Plancia

Deenox Plancia is a private tool. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by its owner, with his own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data we use

  • Gmail (read only): sender, subject, date and text of recent emails, to show the owner which messages need an answer.
  • Gmail (drafts): to save a reply as a draft when the owner asks for it. Deenox Plancia never sends emails: the owner reviews and sends them himself from Gmail.
  • Google Calendar: the list of calendars and their events, to show the owner's agenda on his screens; creating or deleting an event only when the owner asks for it.

Where the data goes

  • The data is stored only on the owner's own computer and home devices.
  • To choose which emails are important, a short list (sender, subject, a short preview) may be sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) on behalf of the owner. It is not used to train models by us and it is not shared with anyone else.
  • We do not sell Google data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not share it with third parties.

Limited Use

Deenox Plancia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. The local copies are deleted by deleting the files on his computer.
