Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Deenox is a personal assistant app made and used by Diego Fanni, known as Doctor Dee (Italy). This policy explains what data Deenox uses when it connects to TikTok.

Who uses Deenox

Deenox is a private tool. It is used only by its owner, with his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and it does not collect data from other TikTok users.

What data we use

When the owner logs in with TikTok, Deenox may receive, only with his permission:

  • basic profile information (display name, avatar, account ID);
  • account statistics (followers, following, total likes, number of videos);
  • the list of his public videos and their numbers (views, likes, comments, shares);
  • permission to upload his own videos to TikTok as drafts.

How we use it

  • To show the owner the results of his own videos on his own computer.
  • To send his finished videos to his TikTok drafts, so he can review and publish them himself.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising.

Where the data is kept

Data is saved only on the owner's personal computer. Access keys (tokens) are stored in a private file and are never published.

How long we keep it

Statistics are kept as long as the owner wants to compare his results over time. They can be deleted at any time.

How to remove access

Access can be removed at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or Manage app permissions). After that, Deenox can no longer read or upload anything. To ask for deletion of any saved data, write to the address below.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
